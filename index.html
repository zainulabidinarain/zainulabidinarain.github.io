About

I am a Computer Systems Engineer specializing in intelligent perception systems for complex physical environments. My research lies at the intersection of computer vision, machine learning, digital signal processing, and mathematical optimization. I use AI not simply to train models, but as a computational tool to solve hard real-world problems involving 3D perception, sensing, physics, and autonomous decision-making.

I am actively seeking graduate research positions (MSc/PhD) in Intelligent Perception and Autonomous Systems.
