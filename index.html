About

I am an early-career researcher working in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Autonomous Systems. My background is in Computer Systems Engineering, but my main focus is on formulating clear research questions and designing systems that solve them reliably.

Currently, I am building multi-agent pipelines for automated scientific discovery, exploring ways to evaluate LLM decisions, and using multimodal learning for complex data like physical signal processing. Instead of just treating AI as an application, I want to understand and improve how these systems reason, execute tasks, and verify information in complex environments.
