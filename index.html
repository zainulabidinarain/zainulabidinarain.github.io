About

I am a Computer Systems Engineer focused on bridging the gap between physical hardware and intelligent software. My research centers on developing efficient, end-to-end perception systems that operate reliably in noisy, real-world environments. Rather than focusing solely on software applications, I am deeply interested in how raw physical data is acquired through sensors, filtered mathematically, and processed directly at the edge.

Currently, my work spans computational perception, signal processing, and robotics. By combining hardware interfacing with lightweight deep learning architectures, my goal is to engineer verifiable and efficient solutions that push the boundaries of automated perception on resource-constrained devices.
